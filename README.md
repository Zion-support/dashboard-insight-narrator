## What it does

Dashboard Insight Narrator reads your BI dashboards and writes clear executive summaries: what changed, why it matters, and what to do next — delivered to Slack, email, or embedded beside the charts.

## Key features

- **Auto-narratives** — every dashboard gets a written summary in plain language.
- **Change explanations** — WoW/MoM deltas with likely drivers surfaced.
- **Audience tuning** — executive, manager, or analyst tone per recipient.
- **Multi-BI support** — works with Looker, Power BI, Tableau, and Metabase.
- **Narrative API** — embed generated insights into your own portals.

## Who it's for

Executives and business teams who want the story behind the charts without digging through dashboards.
